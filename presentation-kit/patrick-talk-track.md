## Language to keep precise

| Say | Keep the distinction |
| --- | --- |
| “Illustrative F-4412 model” | Catalog dimensions are cited; bore, offsets, spacers and application fit remain unverified. |
| “Example quote / local seller response” | NHI has not issued these synthetic terms and no request is sent. |
| “Priced subtotal” | Freight and applicable tax are unpriced, so this is not a final total. |
| “Requested five weeks; proposed six to eight” | A requested date does not become a seller commitment. |
| “Acknowledged review plan” | The engineering question remains open. |
| “Separate Annora platform sample” | Generic motion, Studio and drawing features are not automatically integrated into the NHI host. |

The demo shows an operable experience and preserved context. Improvements in conversion, quote turnaround or sales outcomes remain pilot hypotheses to measure after a real deployment.
